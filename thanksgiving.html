Regional Food Bank

Saturday, November 21, 2026 Starts at 10:00 a.m. First come, first served No pre-registration

Public food distribution at six Orange County sites. Show up early so food is still available. Each household that is served gets a turkey and a bag of groceries.

  • No sign-up. Food is first come, first served.
  • Plan on one household per vehicle. A site can change that limit on the day.
  • Most sites allow walk-ups. Check the food bank page closer to November 21 for the site you will use. Line-up times depend on that town’s rules.
  • This is not home delivery. Meals sent to senior housing are for residents of those buildings only.
